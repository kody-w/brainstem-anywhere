Your AI, in one file.

Download a Brainstem, drop it into claude.ai or any AI that runs Python, and it wakes up there with its own agents and memory.

Download my Brainstem

 

  1. Download

    One file, brainstem.py. No sign-in, no keys inside.

  2. Drop it into your AI

    Attach the file in claude.ai (or any AI that can run Python) and say:

    Wake this Brainstem using the instructions at the top of the file. You are its model. Pass everything I say to it and show me its exact reply.
  3. Talk to it

    Everything you say goes to your Brainstem. Ask it to remember things; its memory agents keep them.

  4. Take it home

    When you're done, ask your AI for chat.json. Put it next to your brainstem.py and run:

    python3 brainstem.py --home chat.json

    Your Brainstem opens on your computer and remembers what it learned. Click Import, choose chat.json, and the conversation carries on.
